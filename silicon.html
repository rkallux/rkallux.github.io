Silicon

Verifying our C2S2 chip

I lead the verification subteam on Cornell Custom Silicon Systems (C2S2). Our SoC was fabricated at TSMC, and after it came back we tested it on the real silicon.

How it went

  1. The chip

    It has a RISC-V core and a 4x4 systolic array, connected through a memory crossbar to on-chip SRAM. The interactive diagram is on the Home page.

  2. Full-chip verification

    I ran verification at the full SoC level, checking that all the blocks work correctly together and match the design spec.

  3. Testing the real chip

    Once the chip came back from TSMC, we loaded programs onto it over SPI and compared what the real chip did against what we expected.
