Silicon

Verifying a chip that came back from TSMC

Most college students never get to test a chip they helped build. On Cornell Custom Silicon Systems (C2S2), I lead the verification subteam, and our SoC was fabricated at TSMC and tested on real silicon.

The story in three steps

  1. One SoC, two brains

    A RISC-V core and a 4x4 systolic array share one chip, connected through a memory crossbar to on-chip SRAM. See the interactive diagram.

  2. I ran verification at the full-SoC level

    Instead of only testing parts one at a time, I checked that the whole chip works together the way the design says it should.

  3. Then we checked it on real silicon

    Once the chip came back from TSMC, we loaded programs onto it over SPI and compared what the real chip did against what we expected.
